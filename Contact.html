WHAT IS FITCORE AND HOW DOES IT WORKS?

Fitcore is a professional grade digital fitness tracking platform.It allows you to log your daily workouts,nutrition plan,calorie intake,set custom goals and visualize progress through real time interactive charts.

HOW DO I TRACK MY DAILY WORKOUTS AND MEALS??

Simply you can navigate to the Dashboard page after logging in...From there, you can select 'Log Workout' to input your sets,reps, and weights or use to 'Log Meal' to track your calorie intake.

HOW DO FITNESS GOALS WORK IN FITCORE?

FitCore provides pre-defined tracking targets like endurance and consistency metrics. You can view your target milestones on the Dashboard, where your daily progress is automatically calculated towards completing those goals.

HOW CAN I REPORT A BUG OR REQUEST A NEW FEATURE?

You can use the Message Us form below on this page or send an email directly to our development team. We regularly update the platform based on user feedback.
